# Theory

These pages explain measurement concepts that apply to every Joulescope, and
to most other test instruments.  They answer common questions about why a
measurement looks the way it does.  For how a specific instrument works, see
the Theory of operation chapter in its user's guide, available from
{doc}`/products/index`.
