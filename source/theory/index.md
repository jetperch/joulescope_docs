# Theory

These pages explain measurement concepts that apply to every Joulescope, and
to most other test instruments.  They answer common questions about why a
measurement looks the way it does.  For how a specific instrument works, see
the Theory of operation chapter in its user's guide, available from
{doc}`/products/index`.

{doc}`bandwidth`
: Why the same event shows a different peak at different sample rates, and
  how the instrument and your circuit each limit what you can see.

{doc}`leakage`
: How current through connected equipment, such as debuggers and logic
  analyzers, changes your sleep current measurements.

{doc}`ground_loops`
: How ground connections between equipment cause noise, ground voltage
  differences and current that bypasses the Joulescope.
