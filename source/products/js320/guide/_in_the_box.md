![JS320 instrument and accessories in the open carry case](media/js320_image8.jpeg)

Your Joulescope JS320 purchase includes:

- Joulescope JS320 instrument with factory calibration

- Binding Post Front Panel, already installed

- 5’ USB 2.0 A to C cable

- USB A to C adapter (not shown above)

- GPIO cable assembly

- 2 mm Allen wrench, for optionally swapping the front panel (not shown above)

- Quick Start Guide

- Factory calibration certificate (not shown above)

- Joulescope sticker

- Carry case

You will need to provide:

- A host computer running one of the {ref}`supported platforms <supported-platforms>`.
  Most computers manufactured within the last five years should work. The
  computer requires an available USB 2.0 port, at least 500 MB of hard drive /
  solid state drive storage space, and 1 GB of available RAM.

- Suitable cable(s) to connect the Joulescope JS320’s binding posts to your
  system, often the power source and device under test.

- Optional BNC cable for connecting trigger to other equipment.

You need an internet connection to download the free software.
