# Joulescope JS320 Datasheet

The Joulescope™ JS320 is a precision DC energy analyzer.  It measures current
and voltage, then computes power, energy and charge.  It measures current
over ten orders of magnitude, from amps down to nanoamps, with fast
autoranging and minimal voltage drop.
