# Joulescope JS110 Datasheet

The Joulescope™ JS110 is a precision DC energy analyzer.  It measures current
and voltage, then computes power and energy.  It combines high-speed sampling
and rapid dynamic current range switching to provide accurate and seamless
current and energy measurements, even for devices with rapidly varying
current consumption.
